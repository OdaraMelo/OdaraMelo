## About

I put AI to work in operations. I build and run AI agents and automations that handle real customer communication, and I review what they write: accuracy, tone and safety. When a reply is wrong, I fix the cause, turn it into a rule or a test, and look for everyone it already affected.

I have worked remotely and independently since 2020. My background also includes biology, quality supervision in an accredited laboratory, university teaching and an MBA.

## What I do

- **Build** workflows and AI agents that connect WhatsApp, e-mail, databases and websites.
- **Evaluate** real conversations: wrong answers, off-tone replies, misclassified messages.
- **Control** quality: an automatic checker runs all day, a guard corrects and rechecks before anything is sent, opt-outs are always respected.
- **Document** each procedure so other people can follow and reuse it.
